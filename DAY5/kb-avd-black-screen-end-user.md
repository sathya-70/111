# My work desktop went black — what do I do?

## Don't worry — your work and files are safe

If your work screen has gone black or disconnected, this is a problem on our side. **Nothing you were working on has been lost.** Your files are saved on our systems and are not affected.

---

## Try these steps first

### Step 1 — Wait a moment, then sign out fully

Do not repeatedly click or press keys. Wait 30 seconds to see if the screen comes back on its own.

If it does not recover, **sign out completely** rather than just closing the window:
1. If you can see the bar at the bottom of the screen, click **Start**, then click your name or account picture, then select **Sign out**.
2. If the screen is black and not reacting, close the work screen window entirely (click the X at the top).

### Step 2 — Wait 2 minutes, then try signing back in

Open the app or website link you normally use to log in to your work screen. Sign in as you would on a normal working day.

### Step 3 — If it happens again, try once more

If you get a black screen again immediately after signing in, sign out again, wait 5 minutes, then try one more time.

This short wait gives our systems time to find you a working connection on their own.

---

## If none of the above works — contact the Service Desk

Please get in touch if:
- The black screen happens every time you try to sign in
- You have tried signing out and back in twice and it keeps failing
- You cannot access your work desktop at all and you need it urgently

**Contact the Service Desk by phone or via the IT support website.**

---

## What to tell the Service Desk

Having these details ready will help us fix the issue faster:

- Your **full name** and **work email address**
- The **date and time** you first noticed the problem
- Whether the screen went black **straight after signing in**, or after working for a while
- How many times you have tried signing back in
- Whether you can see any **message on screen** — if so, read it out or take a photo
- The **device** you are using (e.g. laptop, home PC, work tablet)
- Whether you are working from the **office or from home**

---

*If you are unsure whether this guidance applies to your situation, please contact the Service Desk — we are happy to help.*
