# L1 Self-Service Guide: Device Setup Stuck or Fails

Version: v 1.0, 10/08/2026, status : Draft

Your data is safe. This setup issue does not mean your files were deleted or exposed.

## What this looks like

You are setting up a work device, but setup does not finish and you cannot get to a normal ready-to-work state.

## What you can do yourself

1. Leave the device powered on and connected to the internet for 15 minutes.
2. If nothing changes, restart the device once.
3. Sign in again and try setup one more time.
4. If setup fails again, stop trying repeated restarts and contact the service desk.

## What not to do

1. Do not reset the device yourself.
2. Do not remove work accounts if you are unsure.
3. Do not keep retrying many times in a row.

## Why this can happen

Sometimes the device still has an older work-management connection from earlier use. This can block new setup until support clears it safely.

## Information to include in your ticket

Please share:

1. Your full name and best contact number.
2. Device name (if shown on screen) and asset tag (if available).
3. Date and time the setup failed.
4. The exact message shown on screen (photo is helpful).
5. Whether this is a new device or a reused device.
6. Your office location or whether you are remote.
7. Whether restart and one retry were already done.

Contact the service desk if setup still does not complete after one restart and one retry.
