# Exercise 2 — Proactive Notification: Credentials Vault Access Issue
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Theme:** Credentials Vault Inaccessibility (Rank 1 Priority)

---

**Subject: Update — Shared Password Vault Access Issue**

**To:** Affected teams and their managers
**From:** DWP Endpoint Team
**Date:** 12 August 2026

---

We are aware that a number of colleagues are currently unable to access the shared password vault following last week's update. For those affected, this means they cannot retrieve the shared credentials needed to carry out their normal work, and we understand this is causing real disruption.

**Where we are now**

The issue has been reported by multiple people across at least one team and has been ongoing for several days. We take that seriously. An investigation is underway and we have assigned a dedicated owner to drive this to resolution. We are prioritising it above other post-update issues today.

**What we know so far**

The update appears to have affected how some accounts authenticate to the vault. Not everyone is impacted, but we have not yet confirmed the full scope. We are working to understand exactly who is affected and why, so we can apply the right fix rather than a partial one.

**What is still open**

We do not yet have a resolution time we can commit to. We will share a further update by end of day today, and we will not close this until access has been confirmed as restored for all affected colleagues.

**What to do in the meantime**

If you are unable to access the vault, please log a ticket if you have not already done so — this helps us track scope. If you have an urgent need for a specific credential, contact your line manager who can liaise with the team directly.

We apologise for the disruption and will keep you informed.
