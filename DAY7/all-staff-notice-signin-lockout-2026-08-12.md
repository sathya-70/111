# All-Staff Notice — Sign-In and Account Lockout Issue

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Support Team
**To:** All FinBridge Staff

---

We are aware that a number of colleagues are having difficulty signing in this morning, with some accounts becoming locked after the recent Windows 11 migration. We are actively investigating and working to resolve this as quickly as possible.

**In the meantime, please try the following:**

- Wait 15 minutes before attempting to sign in again — repeated attempts will extend the lockout period.
- After waiting, try signing in once with your usual password.
- If you have recently changed your password, check that any saved passwords on your phone or tablet are updated, as these can trigger lockouts automatically.

If you are still unable to sign in after trying the above, please contact the Service Desk with your name, username, the time you were first locked out, and how many times you attempted to sign in.

We will share a further update by **14:00 today**.

We know this is frustrating and we appreciate your patience. Please avoid logging multiple tickets for the same issue — this helps us focus on the fix.

**IT Support Team**
