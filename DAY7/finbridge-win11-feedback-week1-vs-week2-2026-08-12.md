## New Theme in Week 2 — Not Present in Week 1

---

### Excel Crashing on Large Files
**Week 2 count:** 9 (IDs: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37) | **Severity: Blocker**

This theme did not appear in week 1 feedback. Nine users report Excel crashing or freezing when opening large files, with several citing files over 10MB as the trigger. The finance team is specifically affected, with users losing unsaved work. Volume and urgency of language (multiple "please prioritise" and "urgently" calls) make this the single active Blocker in week 2.

- *"Excel keeps crashing, this is now my biggest issue with the new setup."* (id 30)
- *"Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."* (id 13)
- *"New Excel crash issue – happens specifically with files over 10MB."* (id 33)
