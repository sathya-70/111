# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Self-Service Answers (Top 3 Issues)

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** FinBridge staff — business users

---

## 1. Can't sign in / account locked out

Wait 15 minutes without trying to log in again — repeated attempts will keep your account locked for longer. After waiting, try signing in once using your usual password.

If your password has recently changed, make sure you are typing the new one. Sometimes saved passwords on your phone or tablet can trigger lockouts automatically — check those too.

**Still locked out? Contact the Service Desk.**
Tell them: your name, your username, the time you were first locked out, and how many times you tried to sign in. This helps them unlock your account quickly without needing to ask you lots of questions.

---

## 2. Printer on floor 3 not working

On your keyboard, press the **Windows key**, type **Printers & scanners**, and open it. Check whether the floor 3 printer appears in the list. If it does, click it and select **Set as default**.

If it is not in the list, try signing out of your computer and signing back in — this can trigger the printer to reconnect automatically.

**Still not printing? Contact the Service Desk.**
Tell them: your name, your floor, your computer name (shown in the bottom-right corner of your screen or on a sticker on your device), and whether the printer appears in your Printers & scanners list at all.

---

## 3. OneDrive files appear missing

Open OneDrive by clicking the cloud icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen (near the clock). If it shows a warning or error symbol, click it and select **Resume syncing** or **Sign in** if prompted.

Then open your browser, go to [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.com), and sign in with your work account — your files may still be safely stored online even if they are not yet showing on your device.

**Files still missing after checking online? Contact the Service Desk immediately.**
Tell them: your name, which files or folders are missing, when you last accessed them, and whether you see any error messages in OneDrive. Do not save new files over existing ones until IT confirms your data is safe.
