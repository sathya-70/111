# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Leadership Update

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**To:** Senior Management
**From:** DWP Endpoint Team

---

The Windows 11 migration for FinBridge staff has broadly landed well. The majority of colleagues experienced a smooth transition, and by the end of the first week the most serious issues — staff being unable to sign in, missing files in OneDrive, and an inaccessible shared drive — had all been resolved. VPN reliability, which had been disrupting client calls in week one, is also stable. Positive feedback from staff has increased week on week, and the overall picture is one of a successful migration with expected early turbulence that has largely been worked through.

Two issues remain open and are actively being managed. The floor 3 printer has not mapped correctly since migration day and is now entering its third week unresolved; the affected team is manually walking to another floor to print, which is disruptive and the situation is close to escalation. This is the single most urgent outstanding item and requires a confirmed fix date. Separately, a routine security update applied to finance devices on 4 August has caused noticeably slower login times for that group, and a new pattern of Excel instability when working with large files has emerged among finance staff this week. Both are under investigation. Staff are aware of the Excel issue and have been asked to save work frequently in the meantime. A further update on all three items will be provided by end of day Friday.
