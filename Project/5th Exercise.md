# Partner Update by Lunch - Floor 6 Incident Cluster
Date: 14 Aug 2026  
Audience: Partners and business leads

## What happened
This morning, several people on Floor 6 reported three issues at the same time:
- Some users could see information they should not have seen in Copilot search results.
- Some users had very slow sign-in or failed sign-in.
- Some users lost common desktop shortcuts (like Outlook, Teams, and Edge).

These issues appear linked to recent system and access changes that affected Floor 6 users as a group.

## What is being done now
- Access risk was handled first: the affected Copilot data source has been restricted for Floor 6 while checks are completed.
- Login pressure has been reduced: non-essential device and app changes were paused for affected users.
- Devices are being recovered in waves: sync and restart actions are running in a controlled order.
- Missing shortcuts are being repaired using a scripted fix, then checked by sign-out/sign-in testing.
- Evidence is being collected to confirm exact cause and prevent repeat problems.

## What is still open
- Full confirmation that no users can see restricted Copilot content after all refresh actions complete.
- Final validation that sign-in time has returned to normal for all affected users, not only the first sample group.
- Confirmation that shortcut fixes remain in place after next login cycle.
- Final root-cause sign-off and a permanent prevention plan.

## Current impact and expectation
- Impact remains focused on Floor 6 users.
- Core containment actions are already in place.
- Services are improving, but monitoring is still active until all checks pass.

## Next update
Next partner update will be shared this afternoon with confirmed status, remaining risk, and closure timing.
