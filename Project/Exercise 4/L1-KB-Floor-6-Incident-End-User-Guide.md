# What's Happening on Floor 6? A Quick Guide for You

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

## Don't Worry – Your Data is Safe

We're aware of some technical issues affecting computers on Floor 6 today. We're working to fix them right now. **Your files, emails, and documents are completely safe** – no data has been lost or accessed improperly.

---

## What You Might See

You may experience one or more of these during today:

1. **Slower than usual login** – It might take 5–10 minutes to get to your desktop instead of the usual 2–3 minutes.
2. **Login doesn't work** – You sign in but get an error or the screen freezes.
3. **Missing desktop shortcuts** – Icons for Outlook, Teams, or Edge aren't on your desktop where they normally are.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

### If login is slow or stuck:
- **Wait 5–10 minutes** – Sometimes it just needs extra time.
- **Restart your computer** – Turn it completely off, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on.
- **Try signing out and back in** – If you're logged in but things are stuck, sign out (Start menu > Your name > Sign out), wait 1 minute, then sign back in.

### If shortcuts are missing:
- **Check your Start menu** – Click the Windows logo and search for Outlook, Teams, or Edge. You can still launch them from there.
- **Don't worry** – They're not gone forever; IT will restore them shortly.

### If nothing works:
- **Just wait** – Our team is actively resolving this. It should be better within a couple of hours.

---

## When to Contact the Service Desk

**Call or email IT Service Desk if:**
- You cannot log in after restarting.
- Login is still very slow (over 10 minutes) after waiting.
- You've been waiting more than 2 hours and nothing has improved.
- You see an error message you need help understanding.

---

## Details to Have Ready When You Call

Make it easier for us to help by mentioning:
- **Your computer name** (usually starts with "F6-")
- **When it started** (approximate time)
- **What you see** (slow login, error message, missing shortcuts)
- **What you've already tried** (restart, waited, etc.)
- **Any error messages** you saw (read them exactly if you can)
